Equipment Overview

  • This is a two-station press that secures shoe components in molds and forms or bonds them using pressure and heat.
  • It consists of upper pressing heads, lower molds, a pneumatic system, foot pedals, and an operation panel.
  • Set the actual operating temperature, pressure, and forming time only after confirming the manufacturer's manual.

Abnormal Signs and Actions

Abnormal SignImmediate Action
Head does not descendCheck whether the emergency stop has been reset, the pneumatic supply, foot pedal, and interlocks. If the issue persists, notify maintenance personnel.
Head moves slowlyCheck pneumatic pressure and air leaks. Inspect for clogged filters and cylinder faults.
Pressing force is weakCheck the pressure gauge and regulator setting. Inspect hose leaks and cylinder seals.
Left and right forming quality differsCompare mold position, pad wear, and pressure and temperature settings.
Temperature does not riseCheck the setpoint, heater, sensor, and circuit breaker. Electrical maintenance must be performed by qualified personnel.
Temperature rises excessivelyStop work immediately. Disconnect power and inspect the temperature controller and sensor.
Indentation marks or adhesive residue appears on productsClean the mold and pads. Reset pressure, time, and temperature according to the manufacturer's manual.
Air leakage sound is presentStop the equipment and inspect hoses, fittings, and valves. Replace damaged parts.
Burning smell, smoke, or sparks occurActivate the emergency stop immediately. Disconnect the main power. Do not restart the equipment; notify electrical maintenance personnel.
